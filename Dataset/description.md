Our study employed four publicly available chest CT datasets and one private retrospective dataset. The public datasets included CT_COVID19, Covid19-CT-Scans, 4D-Lung, and QIN-Lung-CT, as detailed in Table 1. These datasets cover diverse pulmonary scenarios, including lung cancer, pneumonia, and healthy cases, as well as scans acquired from different CT devices, providing more comprehensive
data support for the development and generalizability evaluation of the CT quality assessment vision-language model.

Among them, CT_COVID19 and Covid19-CT-Scans are publicly available chest CT datasets released during the COVID-19 pandemic, containing three-dimensional CT data from both patients with COVID-19 pneumonia and healthy individuals. The 4D-Lung and QIN-Lung-CT datasets are both from the Cancer Imaging Archive
(TCIA) in the United States and include three-dimensional CT data from patients with lung cancer, such as non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). HX-Data is a retrospective three-dimensional CT dataset collected at West China Hospital, involving multiple quality-related issues, including incomplete lung coverage, metal artifacts, respiratory motion artifacts, and low-dose noise.
